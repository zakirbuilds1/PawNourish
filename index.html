Questions from
Pet Parents

Can’t find your answer? Call (800) 555-0142 or email hello@pawnourish.com. Our team replies within one business day.

How much is shipping, and how fast is it?

Shipping is free on orders over $50. Below that it’s a flat $5.99. Most orders arrive in 2 to 4 business days.

How do I track my order?

As soon as your order ships we email you a tracking link. You can also call us with your order number and we’ll check it for you.

What if my pet doesn’t like it?

Our Love It Guarantee covers you: tell us within 30 days, even if the bag is open, and we’ll refund you in full or send a different recipe.

How do subscriptions work?

Choose a food and how often it should arrive (every 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks) and you save 15% on every delivery. Skip, pause or cancel anytime with no fees.

Where do your ingredients come from?

Our meat and fish come from farms and fisheries we visit ourselves, and our grains and vegetables are grown in North America. Every recipe is cooked in small batches and tested before it ships.

Is your packaging sustainable?

Our bags are recyclable, our shipping boxes are plastic-free, and we offset the carbon from every delivery.

Are your recipes vet approved?

Yes. Every recipe is formulated with veterinary nutritionists to be complete and balanced for its life stage.
